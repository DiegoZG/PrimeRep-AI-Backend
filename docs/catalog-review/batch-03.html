Steps

  1. Sit on a bench holding dumbbells beside the thighs.
  2. Raise the arms out to the sides toward shoulder height and lower slowly.

Cues

  1. Keep the torso still.

Mistakes

  1. Rocking forward and back to start the raise.

Benefits

Trains shoulder strength through an arm-raising motion with a relatively light load.

Beginner guidance

Begin with a load that lets the arms move without a bounce from the legs.
