Steps

  1. Brace the upper back on a stable bench and hold one padded dumbbell across the hips.
  2. Lower the hips and drive upward until the torso and thighs align.

Cues

  1. Keep the bench from sliding.

Mistakes

  1. Pushing into a large lower-back arch at the top.

Benefits

Builds hip-extension strength with the upper body supported.

Beginner guidance

Learn to finish with the hips extended without trying to lift the ribs higher.
