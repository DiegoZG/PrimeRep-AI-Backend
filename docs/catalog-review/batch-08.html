Steps

  1. Set a high plank with hands under shoulders and feet a little wider than the hips.
  2. Touch one hand to the opposite shoulder, replace it and alternate.

Cues

  1. Keep the hips facing the floor.

Mistakes

  1. Swaying side to side as each hand lifts.

Benefits

Trains deliberate trunk control while the limbs or external resistance challenge position.

Beginner guidance

Shorten the lever or movement range so you can maintain position and continue breathing.
