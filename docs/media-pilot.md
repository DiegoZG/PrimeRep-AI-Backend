## Shared production specification

Produce a 9:16 master showing the full body, contact points and relevant equipment through two or three controlled repetitions. Use a plain, well-lit training space, stable camera, ordinary fitted training clothes, no distracting background people, no on-screen coaching text and no voiceover. Start and end in a stable setup; avoid a loop seam that looks like a changed body or apparatus. Capture enough space that playback needs no cropping.

The first pilot uses one adult participant/model appearance per exercise; appearance and angle variants are media records under the same exercise ID. Record how the participant/model, location and production assets may be used commercially. Select the generation provider and spending limit before paid work begins.

## Candidate briefs

|---|---|---|---|
| `push_up` | Front-side three-quarter view at torso height. Start on hands and toes. Show the chest lowering between the hands and the body pressing back as a coordinated unit. | `dumbbell_shoulder_press` | Seated on an upright supported bench, feet planted, one dumbbell in each hand. Use a front-side view wide enough to show both weights at the top. Show a controlled press from shoulder height and return. | Supported setup, comfortable overhead range, wrist alignment, symmetry and two stable independent dumbbells. | Weights merge, disappear or change shape; wrists bend implausibly; the bench shifts; the person arches excessively; one arm follows a different repetition. |
| `squat` | Three-quarter side view of a barbell back squat inside a rack with correctly placed safeties. Show the whole rack, feet, bar and plates. Include a stable start followed by two or three controlled squats. | Rack/safety placement, bar position, foot contact, knee/hip coordination, controlled depth and consistent plate/bar loading. | The bar passes through the rack/body, plates change count, safeties vanish, feet slide, joints bend implausibly, or the clip teaches an unstable setup. |

These are draft filming/generation directions, not approved technical instruction. The trainer can change the intended range and setup before production. The source exercise IDs and current content revision hashes must be recorded in the candidate manifest.
