Steps

  1. Stand in a long split stance with both feet staying in place.
  2. Bend both knees to lower vertically, then push through the front foot to rise.

Cues

  1. Keep the front heel planted.

Mistakes

  1. Walking the feet along a narrow tightrope line.

Benefits

Builds leg strength while controlling one side of the body at a time.

Beginner guidance

Practise a shallow bodyweight version and use a stable support if balance limits control.

Steps

  2. Lower through the front hip and knee, then stand while the rear foot provides support.

Cues

Mistakes

Builds leg strength while controlling one side of the body at a time.
