Steps

  1. Place the rear foot on a low stable bench and hold dumbbells beside the hips.
  2. Lower through the front hip and knee, then stand while the rear foot provides support.

Cues

  1. Use a bench height that permits balance.

Mistakes

  1. Pushing mostly through the rear leg.

Benefits

Builds leg strength while controlling one side of the body at a time.

Beginner guidance

Practise a shallow bodyweight version and use a stable support if balance limits control.
